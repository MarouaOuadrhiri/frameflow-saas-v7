Safi, l'admin 3ndek ma khdemch (Access is denied — t-taqa mazal machi admin). Bla ma nday3o l'w9t, kayn 7el bla admin kaydir nafs l'gharad: Windows kaykhdem ay haja f dossier Startup fo9ma katdkhel l'session.

1. 7el PowerShell 3adi (bla admin), w sir l scripts:



cd "C:\Users\Mediast 01\Documents\New folder\frameflow-saas-v7\scripts"
2. Colli hadchi:



$startup = [Environment]::GetFolderPath('Startup')
$vbs = Join-Path (Get-Location) 'run-silent.vbs'
$sc = (New-Object -ComObject WScript.Shell).CreateShortcut("$startup\FrameFlow.lnk")
$sc.TargetPath = "wscript.exe"
$sc.Arguments = "`"$vbs`""
$sc.Save()
Test-Path "$startup\FrameFlow.lnk"
Ila tba3 True → safi, l'raccourci tkhleq.

3. Daba restart l'PC (w dkhel l'session dyalk), tsenna 1-2 min, w 7el http://project-management — khassha tkhdem bo7dha.

4. Test:

schtasks /Run /TN "FrameFlowSilentStart"
